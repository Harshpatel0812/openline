# Sprints 1–2 review — Foundation + Contacts (built Nov 13–14, 2026)

Goal: a team can sign up, invite colleagues, and import and manage contacts, with tenant isolation
enforced by the database. Met: **yes** (one acceptance criterion deferred, see below).

## Shipped

- **Auth (ADR 0003):** sign-up, login, logout, sessions (14-day sliding, 90-day absolute), workspace
  switching, login returns you to your last workspace. scrypt passwords, rate limits, CSRF header,
  no account enumeration (same error and similar timing).
- **Team:** members list, role changes (owner rules, last-owner protection with row locks), remove
  member (ends their sessions, revokes their pending invites), invites with single-use 7-day links.
- **Contacts:** create, edit, delete (+ optional suppression), search, keyset pagination, companies
  auto-created from email domains (free-mail excluded).
- **CSV import:** preview before saving (counts, column mapping, invalid rows), BOM/CRLF/semicolon/
  tab support, skip or update duplicates, lawful-basis confirmation, 5 MB / 10,000-row limits.
- **Suppressions:** email or whole-domain entries; unsubscribe entries can't be removed.
- **Database (ADR 0002):** Drizzle + SQL migrations; row-level security on every tenant table, split
  read/write policies; the app role can't bypass RLS, delete workspaces or alter the audit log.
- **Web app:** landing, sign-up, login, invite acceptance, app shell, contacts, import wizard,
  companies, team and suppression settings; accessible components; nonce-based CSP.
- **Agents:** `docs/roles/` (7 roles) and `docs/agents.md`.
