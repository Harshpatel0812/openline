# Sprint 5 review — Promise Keeper and Compliance Copilot

Goal: no forgotten promises, and a clear, recorded answer to "can I email this person now?".
Met: **yes**. Spec: `docs/product/OL-E6-promise-keeper-and-compliance-copilot.md`.

## Shipped

- **Tasks:** a task list (`/app/tasks`: overdue, today, upcoming, done; mine or the team's for
  managers), task cards on Today, an overdue badge in the sidebar, tasks on each contact.
- **Promise Keeper:** reads human replies and the replies reps send, finds commitments on either
  side ("I'll send the pricing by Friday", "try me after budget in March", "I'll get back to you
  next week") and suggests a dated follow-up task. Dates are worked out in code from the email's
  own words and date, never by the model. Nothing is created until someone clicks Accept (the
  mailbox owner or a manager). Without the AI, or over budget, conservative English phrase rules
  take over. "Looks like you sent it — mark done?" when a rep replies after promising something.
- **Decision:** Promise Keeper suggests follow-up tasks only. It never pauses or restarts
  sequences (a reply already stops them).
- **Compliance Copilot:** one deterministic rule engine (`packages/compliance/evaluate.ts`) answers
  Allowed / Not right now / Blocked with each check, a plain-language reason and where the rule
  comes from (CAN-SPAM, CASL, GDPR, suppression list, contact status, business hours as an
  Openline policy). The sending engine, inbox replies, the enroll preview and the contact page all
  use it, so they always agree.
- **Proof:** every email Openline sends stores the checks it passed (ruleset version, decision,
  time; no message body). The database refuses to change or delete a stored proof. Managers can
  download a contact's full compliance record.
