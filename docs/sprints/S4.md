# Sprint 4 review — Replies, reply inbox and Scout v1

Goal: no follow-up after someone replies; every reply in one labelled inbox; Scout drafts emails
with sources that a person approves. Met: **yes**. Spec: `docs/product/OL-E4-replies-and-scout.md`.

## Shipped

- **Reply detection** for SMTP mailboxes (IMAP, login checked first) and Gmail (read access asked
  for only when the user turns it on). Checked every 2 minutes by the worker, or by the API when it
  runs the scheduler.
- **What a reply does:** a human reply stops every active sequence for that contact (a colleague at
  the same company counts); an out-of-office pauses until the return date; a verified bounce marks
  the contact bounced; "remove me" suppresses the sender. Newsletters and notifications are ignored.
- **Labels:** interested, not now, referral, not interested, unsubscribe, out of office, bounce,
  other. Header rules first, then Scout's classifier within its budget, else keyword rules (shown
  as "(rule)"). Reps can change a label.
- **Reply inbox** (`/app/inbox`): needs-action view, filters, plain-text detail, threaded reply
  from the same mailbox with every compliance check, sidebar badge and a Today card.
- **Scout v1:** first-touch and reply drafts with a source for every claim about the prospect
  (claims citing sources Scout wasn't given are rejected); review panel to edit, approve or reject;
  nothing is sent by Scout; every decision audited. Website research (3 pages, SSRF-guarded,
  cached 7 days). Workspace switch, monthly credits, web-research toggle, "About us" and value
  proposition.
- **Model access:** AWS Bedrock in production (zero retention), direct API for local development,
  a fake provider for tests (refused outside development/test). Prompts and outputs are never
  logged (ADR 0005).

Notable fixes from the reviews: a polite reply in another language that quoted our footer could
have unsubscribed the contact; forged bounce reports could have marked contacts bounced; an admin
could have pointed a rep's stored login at another server; an out-of-office during a send lost its
pause; Gmail could skip messages after an interrupted sync.
