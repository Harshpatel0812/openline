# Sprint 7 review — CRM sync (HubSpot and Pipedrive)

Goal: teams that already pay for HubSpot or Pipedrive stop typing everything twice, and an opt-out
in one tool holds in the other. Met: **yes, on the fake CRMs**; the real apps still need to be
registered (go-live steps below). Spec: `docs/product/OL-E8-crm-sync.md`, ADR 0006.

## Shipped

- **Connect:** owners and admins connect one CRM per workspace from Settings → Integrations
  (OAuth with a signed, 10-minute state; minimal scopes; tokens sealed, never shown or logged).
  Staging accepts only listed sandbox accounts; production only the live CRMs. Disconnect revokes
  Openline's access and deletes nothing in either system.
- **Setup wizard:** pick the CRM pipeline, map stages both ways (pre-filled by name), map fields
  (standard rows plus up to 20 custom ones per record type, each with a direction and a "who wins"
  rule), then a **preview** that writes nothing and shows what will be created, linked, updated or
  skipped, then the import with the lawful-basis checkbox.
- **Two-way sync:** contacts, companies and deals every 10 minutes (HubSpot) or 15 (Pipedrive),
  backing off to 30 when quiet, and within a minute of an edit in Openline. Only real conflicts are
  decided (per field, newest change wins unless the field is pinned) and each is logged without
  values. Our own writes never echo back.
- **Opt-outs travel, re-subscribes never do:** an Openline unsubscribe opts the person out in the
  CRM; a CRM opt-out becomes a locked Openline suppression. Suppressed people are never imported or
  pushed.
- **Deletes and merges are soft:** "Deleted in HubSpot" keeps the Openline record; erased people
  leave a tombstone so they never come back; merges re-link by email or domain and never merge
  Openline records by themselves.
- **Sync log and badges:** runs and per-record errors (retry, dismiss) for managers and up;
  "Synced with HubSpot · 4 min ago" on contact, company and deal pages and rows.
- **Limits:** HubSpot rate limits and the Pipedrive daily token budget are respected; Pipedrive
  spend is capped per connection ("Paused until tomorrow: daily API budget").
- Help article "CRM sync", pricing and feature pages updated, runbook for the CRM apps.

What the review fixes changed, in short:

- One bad record can no longer stop a whole import or sync. Two CRM companies with the same domain
  (gamma.io and www.gamma.io), or a CRM edit to a domain another company already has, now leave
  that one record with a "Domain already used by another company" error; everything else syncs.
  A run that fails is always marked failed (never left "running"), and an import resumes.
- Domain suppressions (`@acme.com`) now count everywhere: such people are not imported, not
  pushed, and their opt-outs are sent to the CRM.
- A CRM opt-out on someone with an ordinary (unlocked) suppression now locks it and always marks
  the contact unsubscribed.
- After a move to a plan without CRM sync, nothing syncs (it resumes on upgrade), but the
  connection still shows with an upgrade note and can be paused or disconnected.
- A stage added later on either side now shows "Stage mapping needs attention", an error row and
  paused deal badges, instead of deals silently not syncing.
- Field mappings can't use the email or opt-out fields, or one CRM field twice.
- Pipedrive edits within the same second are compared fairly (its clock has whole seconds; ties go
  to the CRM).
- An Openline edit reaches the CRM within minutes even when polling has slowed down.
- Domains are stored without "www." so companies match; importing again after "forget links"
  matches deals by exact name instead of copying them; "forget links" keeps erased people erased,
  and the dialog says so.
- Preview counts now match the import, Pipedrive spend is saved on every path and matches what
  Pipedrive charged, and a preview that ran out of time says its counts are partial.
- A disconnect or Pipedrive uninstall during a sync always wins: the sync stops before its next
  write and never writes the tokens or status back. The uninstall check compares credentials in
  constant time; record links only go to HubSpot and Pipedrive hosts; Sync now, retries and failed
  or refused connects are audited.

## Not shipped / later

- Real HubSpot and Pipedrive apps: not registered yet (go-live steps below); everything was built
  and tested against fake CRM servers.
- Webhooks (near real-time changes), several pipelines, Salesforce, tasks / calls / meetings,
  deleting or merging in the CRM, pushing extra deal contacts (ADR 0006: v2).
- The activity note's HubSpot scope is to be confirmed on the real app (PRD, H9).
- Rate limits for Sync now and setup reads are kept in memory per API instance; they move to Redis
  with the other limits when we run more than one instance.

## Go-live steps for Harsh

Step by step, no coding: `docs/runbooks/crm-apps-setup.md` (about 1 h 30).

1. Register the **HubSpot** apps (staging and production) and create a HubSpot test account.
2. Register the **Pipedrive** apps (staging and production) in a Pipedrive developer sandbox.
3. Put the four client ids and secrets per environment into **AWS Secrets Manager**
   (`openline/staging/crm`, `openline/production/crm`) and send the engineer only the secret names
   and the sandbox account ids.
